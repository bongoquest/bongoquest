There is a unique, heavy kind of quiet that exists between people who have a lot to say, but choose to say nothing at all. It is a space filled with unspoken feelings—words trapped right at the edge of the lips, glances that linger a second too long, and thoughts that echo loudly in the silence.

Whether it is a parting of ways, a suppressed confession of love, or an unresolved tension that hangs in the air, the emotion behind what remains unsaid often carries more weight than any dialogue ever could.
